## Manage AI Memory

In **Profile → AI Memory**, you can inspect your active memories, add a memory, edit it, view why it is remembered, forget one memory, or forget a category. Viewing a screen, product, scan, Weather, Calendar Context, or Profile does not automatically save a persistent AI Memory.

Use **Clear Temporary AI Context** to clear temporary context without deleting saved AI Memory. Use **Clear All AI Memories** to remove AI Memory only; it preserves Profile, Saved Scans, scan images, Closet items, favorites, shopping lists, and the account.

## Export Local Data

Use the app’s existing local-data export control where available. The export includes applicable active local AI Memory records and excludes credentials, tokens, device identifiers, raw images, raw OCR, raw Calendar events, internal routing identifiers, and diagnostics.

## Delete Local Data or Request Account Deletion

Use **Profile → Data Control → Delete My Saved Data** to delete supported local customer data from the device. This does not by itself confirm deletion of server-side account data. Use an available account-deletion flow or contact **hello@goodnessoflifeandfun.com** for support.

## Permission Choices

You can revoke Camera, Calendar, Location, Microphone, or Speech Recognition permissions in iOS Settings. Calendar Context is optional: when enabled with full Calendar access, StyleMatch reads a bounded upcoming-event window locally and reduces relevant events to a minimized styling context. It does not edit your Apple Calendar. Disabling or disconnecting Calendar Context stops future app-side Calendar projection; it does not change your Apple Calendar.

## Social, Support, and Shopping Choices

Style Friends sharing, friend requests, direct messages, reactions, reports,
and blocks are explicit actions. Do not include sensitive information in
shared looks, messages, or reports. AI Customer Service is separate from
direct messages and requires its own consent. Shop AI can use the relevant
context you choose to submit; retailer links open outside StyleMatch Pro under
the retailer's own policy.
