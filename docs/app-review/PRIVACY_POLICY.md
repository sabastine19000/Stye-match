# StyleMatch Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

StyleMatch Pro ("StyleMatch Pro," "we," "us," or "our") provides outfit scanning, wardrobe organization, styling assistance, weather-aware recommendations, and links to clothing sold by third-party retailers. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Contact: [hello@goodnessoflifeandfun.com](mailto:hello@goodnessoflifeandfun.com)

## Information handled by StyleMatch Pro

Depending on the features you choose to use, StyleMatch Pro may handle:

- Account and authentication information, including an account identifier and authentication/session information used for Sign in with Apple.
- Messages you send to the AI Stylist and the conversation context needed to answer them.
- Scan-derived outfit information, including the authoritative StyleMatch score, detected style information, colors, patterns, fit observations, accessories, and saved scan results.
- Style preferences, clothing sizes and measurements you enter, closet items, favorite brands or stores, occasions, budget preferences, wish-list items, saved products, and shopping interactions.
- Weather and location context used to provide weather-aware styling, such as location supplied to Apple Weather and weather conditions included in a styling request.
- Technical request information needed to provide and protect the service, such as a pseudonymous device or account identifier, request status, bounded counts, and error codes.

## On-device information

Outfit photos are analyzed on your device. The production AI request path does not send outfit photos, Base64-encoded images, or image URLs to StyleMatch Pro's backend or OpenAI. Scan history, closet information, preferences, and AI conversation history may also be stored locally on your device so the app can provide its features.

Information processed only on your device is not sent to StyleMatch Pro merely because it is stored or analyzed locally. When you explicitly allow AI data sharing and request an AI feature, relevant text-based context may be transmitted as described below.

## AI Stylist and third-party AI processing

Before StyleMatch Pro sends personal information for an external AI request, the app explains what will be sent, identifies OpenAI, and asks for your permission. If you allow it, StyleMatch Pro may send your messages and relevant text-based styling context—such as saved scan results, outfit details, style preferences, sizes, closet items, budget, occasion, shopping context, and weather context—to the StyleMatch Pro backend and OpenAI to generate the requested response.

StyleMatch Pro uses Cloudflare infrastructure to receive and route the request to OpenAI. OpenAI is the third-party AI recipient. OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in, and that API inputs and outputs may be retained for up to 30 days for service delivery and abuse monitoring unless a different approved retention control applies or longer retention is legally required. See [OpenAI's business data privacy information](https://openai.com/business-data/) and [enterprise privacy information](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/) for current details.

StyleMatch Pro does not use AI responses to replace, recompute, or override the authoritative outfit score. The AI may explain existing results and offer styling suggestions.

## Weather and location

Apple Weather, provided through WeatherKit, is the app's sole weather-data provider. Location is used to request current conditions and forecasts from Apple Weather. Weather-derived text context may be included in an AI Stylist request only after you grant the external AI consent described above. Apple Weather attribution and Apple's legal attribution page are displayed wherever Apple Weather information appears.

## Shopping and affiliate links

StyleMatch Pro can show clothing and physical products offered by third-party retailers. When you choose a retailer link, you leave StyleMatch Pro and the retailer's own privacy policy applies. StyleMatch Pro may receive an affiliate commission from a qualifying physical-goods purchase, at no additional cost to you. The app does not sell paid digital content, subscriptions, or feature unlocks, and it does not process retailer payment-card information.

## How information is used

We use information to:

- provide account, scan, closet, shopping, weather, and AI Stylist functionality;
- personalize styling suggestions you request;
- preserve your saved preferences and results;
- authenticate requests, enforce service limits, and protect the service;
- diagnose failures using privacy-safe event names, bounded counts, and error classifications; and
- comply with legal obligations and respond to valid requests.

We do not use this information for cross-app tracking, sell it to data brokers, or use it for third-party advertising.

## Service providers

- **Cloudflare** provides network, Worker, and backend infrastructure used to route requests and operate StyleMatch Pro services. Cloudflare processes requests under its applicable privacy and data-processing terms.
- **OpenAI** receives the text-based information described in the AI section to generate AI Stylist responses after you consent.
- **Apple** provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Weather. Apple's privacy terms govern information Apple processes through those services.
- **Retailers and affiliate partners** receive information when you deliberately open their websites or use their services.

## Retention

StyleMatch Pro retains locally saved information on your device until you remove it through the app, delete local customer data, delete the app, or the operating system removes the app's data. The StyleMatch Pro backend retains account authentication records and sessions as needed to provide account access; sessions are time-limited, and account deletion removes the associated account and session records after required reauthentication. A short-lived deletion receipt may be retained to make deletion requests safely repeatable.

StyleMatch Pro does not intentionally persist the contents of AI prompts or AI responses in its backend. Your device may retain AI conversation history locally. Cloudflare and OpenAI may process or retain service data under their own applicable terms, including the OpenAI API practices described above.

We do not state a universal retention period for all provider operational records because those periods are controlled by the provider's current terms and legal requirements.

## Your choices and rights

- **Decline AI data sharing:** Scanning, saved results, Closet, and Shopping remain available. External AI Stylist features will not send a request.
- **Withdraw AI consent:** Use Profile > AI Data Sharing > Withdraw AI Consent. Future external AI requests are blocked unless you grant the current disclosure again.
- **Delete local data:** Use the app's privacy controls to delete saved profile, closet, assistant, shopping, and scan-history information from the device.
- **Delete your account:** Use Profile > Delete Account. Recent Sign in with Apple confirmation may be required before the backend account is deleted.
- **Location:** You can change location permission in iOS Settings. If current Apple Weather data is unavailable, the app uses valid recent data or shows weather as unavailable rather than inventing conditions.
- **Contact us:** Email [hello@goodnessoflifeandfun.com](mailto:hello@goodnessoflifeandfun.com) to ask a privacy question or request assistance with access or deletion.

Your rights may vary by location. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

## Security

We use platform security features and access controls intended to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's privacy

StyleMatch Pro is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its service providers change. We will update the date above and, when appropriate, provide notice in the app. A material change to the categories shared with external AI requires renewed in-app consent before another external AI request.
